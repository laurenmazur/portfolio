about me

Born in Boston and raised in Scottsdale, Arizona, I moved to Cork, Ireland, for school and university before heading to Dublin to start my career at Accenture. Three years later, I traded Dublin for New York and took a bite out of the Big Apple. I'm now a Senior Consultant at CrossCountry Consulting, working with asset management clients to implement new technologies and platforms.

Outside of work, I'm usually running, training at The Yard Gym, hunting down an iced latte, walking someone else's dog, reading, scrolling Pinterest or filming and editing videos to feed my creative side. I'm a people person who thrives on being around others, bringing people together and always having something on the go. Add in some stereotypical eldest-daughter energy, Type A tendencies and an overambitious to-do list, and I'm pretty much in my element.

what i do

Right now I lead operational and implementation strategy for a $25M enterprise technology transformation, and I built an AI-powered dashboard that tracks growth opportunities and business development performance. I also support go-to-market and growth for an early-stage consumer brand, and I'm happiest where strategy meets something creative.
