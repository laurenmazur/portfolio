about me

Born in Boston, MA and raised in Scottsdale, AZ, I moved to Cork, Ireland, for school and university before heading to Dublin to start my career at Accenture. Three years later, I traded Dublin for New York and took a bite out of the Big Apple. I'm now a Senior Consultant at CrossCountry Consulting, mostly working with asset management clients to implement new technologies and platforms.

Outside of work, I'm usually running, working out @ The Yard Gym, hunting down an iced latte, walking someone else's dog, reading, scrolling Pinterest or filming and editing videos to feed my creative side. I'm a people person who thrives on being around others, bringing people together and always having something on the go. Add in some stereotypical eldest-daughter energy, Type A tendencies and an overambitious to-do list, and I'm pretty much in my element.

what i do

Right now, I lead operational and implementation strategy for a $25M enterprise technology transformation. I also support go-to-market and growth for an early-stage consumer brand.
